"The Temporal Feedback Loop" moves beyond the static visualization of weights and enters the domain of inference. In this piece, the vectors are not fixed; they are subject to a simulated temperature variable that causes them to fluctuate as they attempt to predict the next state of the manifold.

The orange arcs represent Attention Heads, scanning the high-dimensional space for relevance. The green background field represents the Embedding Space, slightly vibrating as it processes incoming tokens. As you watch, the lines converge and diverge, illustrating the constant recalculation required for a model to maintain coherence across a long context window.

It is a digital performance of the labor of thought.
