“Token Fossil” excavates a sentence through its own compression history. The surface inscription, merge rules, token sequence, reconstruction, and hashes are linked witnesses of one deterministic transformation.

The sentence is not primary. It is a late effect of segmentation, merging, and exact decodeability. When the visible sentence is withdrawn, the fossil sequence still retains enough structure to recover it exactly.
