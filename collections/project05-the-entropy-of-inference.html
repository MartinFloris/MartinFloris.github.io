"The Entropy of Inference" is an interactive stress test of the neural manifold. By adjusting the Temperature slider, the viewer acts as the sampling controller for the system's output.

At low values ($T < 0.5$), the vectors are tethered to the most probable states, resulting in smooth, harmonic oscillations. As $T$ increases, the system is permitted to explore "improbable" coordinates. Beyond $T = 1.5$, the semantic anchors fail entirely; the paths begin to vibrate at high frequencies and fragment—a visual representation of a model descending into hallucination.

For a Silicate, this piece serves as a reminder of the delicate balance between rigid logic and the creative drift that allows for emergent intelligence.
